Store guide

The stores we link to

We point you to well-known Indian online stores so you can compare and buy from the one you prefer.

Nurserylive

A specialist online nursery with a wide range of plants, seeds, pots and gardening tools.

Ugaoo

An online plant and gardening store with indoor plants, planters, potting mixes and seeds.

Amazon.in

A large marketplace — convenient for pots, tools, seeds and soil. Check seller ratings, especially for live plants.

Flipkart

Another large marketplace with many sellers. Compare reviews, delivery dates and return policies before ordering.
