Light guide

How to judge the light in your space

Light is the most important factor in choosing a plant. Use this quick guide when answering question 2.

Low light

Rooms or corners far from windows, or north-facing rooms. You can read comfortably, but there are no shadows. Try snake plant or ZZ plant.

Medium light

Within a couple of metres of a window, with soft shadows and no direct sun on the leaves. Most foliage houseplants are happy here.

Bright indirect light

Close to a bright window, with lots of light but little direct sun on the plant — often behind a sheer curtain.

Direct sun

Four or more hours of direct sunlight, like a south- or west-facing balcony. Ideal for hibiscus, bougainvillea, tulsi and succulents.
