About GreenCorner

We're a plant & gardening hub on a simple mission: help more people choose, buy and grow healthy plants at home.

Our story

From one crowded balcony to a plant & gardening hub

GreenCorner began with a familiar problem: a small city flat, a love of plants and a lot of conflicting advice. Some plants thrived; many didn't. Most of the losses came down to the same few things — the wrong light, too much water and pots with nowhere for that water to go.

We built GreenCorner to make plant care simpler. We recommend plants and essentials that suit Indian homes, explain what each one actually needs, and point you to trusted online stores — Nurserylive, Ugaoo, Amazon.in and Flipkart — so you can compare and buy with confidence. Our free guides and tools help with the rest.

How we work

From research to your windowsill

  1. Research

    We shortlist plants and supplies that are easy to find in India and suit local homes and seasons.

  2. Explain the care

    Each pick lists its light, watering and care needs in plain language.

  3. Point you to stores

    We link to Nurserylive, Ugaoo, Amazon.in and Flipkart so you can compare and buy.

  4. Support at home

    Our guides and the Care Planner help your plant settle in after it arrives.

Sustainability

Growing greener, responsibly

We favour peat-free growing media such as cocopeat and compost, encourage reusing nursery pots, and suggest durable tools and planters that last for years.

We also encourage fewer, healthier plants over impulse buys — a thriving plant is the most sustainable plant.
