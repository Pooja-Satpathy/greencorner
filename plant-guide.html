Quick tips

Six plant care tips that work everywhere

1

Check the soil, not the calendar

Water when the top few centimetres feel dry. Schedules ignore season, light and pot size.

2

Always use drainage holes

Excess water must escape. Use decorative pots as covers around nursery pots.

3

Match the plant to your light

Low-light corners suit snake and ZZ plants; flowering plants need direct sun.

4

Water deeply, then drain

Soak the whole root ball until water runs out, then empty the saucer.

5

Feed lightly in the growing season

Diluted fertiliser every few weeks in warm months; pause in winter.

6

Inspect new plants

Check leaf undersides for pests and keep new arrivals apart for a week or two.
