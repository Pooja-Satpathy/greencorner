      Using the Plant Care Planner

      1

      Add your plants

      Pick a plant from our catalogue to pre-fill a typical watering interval, or add your own.

      2

      Record watering

      Tap “Watered today” and the next watering date updates automatically.

      3

      Log other care

      Keep notes on fertilising, pruning, repotting and pest checks.

      4

      Get reminders

      See what's due on this page, enable browser alerts, or add a repeating reminder to your calendar.

      Reminders are approximate

      Plants dry faster in summer and slower in winter or the monsoon. Always check the soil before watering — see our watering guide.

      Private by design

      Your records are stored only in this browser using localStorage. Clearing browser data removes them, so download a backup now and then.
