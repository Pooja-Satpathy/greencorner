FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does GreenCorner sell plants?

No. GreenCorner is a plant care and recommendation site. Each pick links to Nurserylive, Ugaoo, Amazon.in and Flipkart so you can compare and buy directly from the store you prefer. We are not affiliated with these stores and don't earn commission.

Which store should I buy from?

Compare price, plant size, delivery to your pin code and the return policy. Specialist plant stores often describe plant size and care in more detail, while large marketplaces are convenient for pots, tools, seeds and soil — check seller ratings and recent reviews, especially for live plants.

How often should I water my new plant?

It depends on the plant, the light and the season. As a rule, water when the top 2–3 cm of soil is dry, and much less often for succulents, snake plants and ZZ plants. Every pick lists an approximate watering frequency, and our watering guide explains how to check the soil.

Should I repot my plant as soon as it arrives?

No. Let a new plant adjust to your home for one to two weeks first. Repot only when roots fill the pot, moving up just one size (about 2–5 cm wider).

Which plants are safe for homes with pets?

Spider plants and areca palms are listed as non-toxic to cats and dogs by the ASPCA, while snake plants, money plants (pothos), peace lilies, jade plants and aloe vera are listed as toxic. Each pick includes a pet note where relevant — always check before buying.
